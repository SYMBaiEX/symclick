# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
constructive, and considerate. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks,
harassment, discrimination, doxxing, and sexualized or threatening conduct are not.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and communications
that violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. Retaliation against
someone who reports a concern is itself a violation.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
